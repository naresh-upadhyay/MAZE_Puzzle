## 📈 Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) Guidelines for Visuals

1. **App Icon (512x512 PNG)**:
   - High-contrast 3D glowing maze cell or glowing ball exiting a neon portal against a dark background.
   - Avoid small text inside the icon; focus on clean visual branding.

2. **Feature Graphic (1024x500 PNG/JPEG)**:
   - Bold title text: "3D NEON MAZE ADVENTURE".
   - Show dynamic 3D gameplay perspective with neon blue/magenta glow effects.

3. **Screenshots (Minimum 4, Recommended 6-8)**:
   - Screenshot 1: **"Conquer 3D Neon Mazes"** (Show main gameplay).
   - Screenshot 2: **"Infinite Procedural Levels"** (Highlight Simple vs Complicated modes).
   - Screenshot 3: **"Unlock Cyber Neon Themes"** (Showcase Theme Store / molten lava / cyber ocean).
   - Screenshot 4: **"100% Offline Play & Boosters"** (Highlight Undo & Hint powers).
   - Screenshot 5: **"150+ Stages & Global Leaderboard"** (Show World Map & Achievements).
